# Client Intake Form — Agentic Business Audit

Use this before designing agents. The goal is not to ask “what should we automate?” but to understand how the operation actually works.

## Short client-facing message

```text
To identify where AI agents can help, I need to understand your current operations: repeated work, bottlenecks, tools, data, and approval points.

Please answer briefly. Bullet points or voice notes are fine. The more honestly you describe manual work and friction, the better the automation map will be.
```

## Minimum intake — 10 questions

1. What does the business sell and to whom? B2B/B2C, average check, main product/service.
2. Where do leads/orders/requests come from? Website, Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram, calls, partners, CRM, other.
3. What happens after the first request? Describe the path from first message to payment, delivery, or loss.
4. Which 3-5 processes repeat every week and take the most time?
5. Where is the main bottleneck: response speed, request quality, lost leads, CRM, documents, support, reports, content, employee control?
6. Which tools are used: CRM, spreadsheets, messengers, email, Notion, accounting, warehouse, telephony, task tracker, payment, website?
7. Where is the source of truth for clients, deals, tasks, documents, instructions, knowledge base, and finances?
8. What examples can be shared for analysis: chats, CRM export, spreadsheets, SOPs, reports, sales scripts, FAQ?
9. What must an agent never do without human approval: message clients, change CRM, issue invoice, give discount, publish, delete, promise deadlines?
10. What result in 2-4 weeks would make the pilot clearly useful: less manual work, faster response, more qualified leads, cleaner CRM, daily report, fewer errors?

## Deep intake

### Business model

- What products/services and customer segments exist?
- How many leads/orders/tickets arrive per week?
- How long is the sales/delivery cycle?
- Who makes the buying decision?
- Where is time or money most often lost?

### Team and responsibilities

- Who is on the team and what does each role own?
- Which roles are overloaded with routine work?
- Where does a manager need control or approval?
- Which actions depend on one key person?

### Process map

For each core process:

```text
Process name:
Owner:
Trigger:
Input data:
Steps:
Tools:
Output/result:
Weekly frequency:
What breaks / annoys people:
Which mistakes are expensive:
```

### Tools and data

- Do CRM/spreadsheets/website/telephony tools have API, webhooks, or export?
- Are there standard message/document templates?
- Are call recordings or transcripts available?
- Is there a knowledge base, FAQ, or SOP set?
- Which data must not be sent to external AI services?

### Approval and risk

- What can be automated fully?
- What can be automated only as draft/checklist?
- Which actions need manager approval?
- What would cause reputational, legal, or financial damage if the agent makes a mistake?

## Red flags

- “Everything is in managers' heads.” Capture knowledge first.
- “We want the agent to sell and close by itself.” Start with qualification/draft workflow.
- “No CRM, but we want analytics.” Establish source of truth first.
- “Mistakes are impossible.” Use human-in-the-loop and narrow scope.
- “Automate everything.” Run process audit before implementation.
