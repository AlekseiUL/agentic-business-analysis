# Agentic Business Analysis — Example Patterns

Use these as pattern memory, not as fake case studies. Adapt to real client facts.

## 1. Online school / expert education

### Typical process lanes

- Lead from Telegram/Instagram/webinar.
- Qualification in DM or CRM.
- Sales call booking.
- Payment/check access.
- Student onboarding.
- Support questions.
- Content production.
- Weekly progress/reporting.

### Good first pilots

**Lead Intake + Qualification Agent**

- Reads: Telegram/WhatsApp/site forms.
- Extracts: name, contact, interest, level, goal, urgency, objection.
- Writes: CRM card/draft update.
- Approval: manager approves answer/call booking.
- KPI: time to first structured response, fewer lost leads.

**Student Support Triage Agent**

- Reads: support chat + FAQ + course rules.
- Drafts answers for top questions.
- Escalates payment, conflict, medical, legal, or personal cases.
- KPI: fewer repetitive support messages handled manually.

**Content Repurposing Agent**

- Reads: webinars, voice notes, posts, Q&A.
- Produces: post drafts, lesson summaries, FAQ updates.
- Approval: editor/founder approves before publishing.

### Avoid first

- Agent independently selling high-ticket programs.
- Agent promising outcomes/certification/results.
- Full autonomous student assessment if consequences are serious.

## 2. Real estate agency

### Typical process lanes

- Incoming buyer/seller/rent requests.
- Object matching.
- Showing scheduling.
- Document collection.
- Follow-ups.
- Market monitoring.
- CRM status control.

### Good first pilots

**Request Structuring Agent**

- Extracts budget, location, rooms, timing, mortgage/cash, must-haves.
- Creates CRM card or sends manager-ready summary.
- Asks missing questions.
- KPI: fewer incomplete requests and faster qualification.

**Listing Match Assistant**

- Reads client criteria + listing database/export.
- Suggests matching objects.
- Human approves before sending.
- Risk: outdated listings/prices. Needs source refresh.

**Follow-up Control Agent**

- Detects stale leads and missed next actions.
- Sends internal reminders.
- KPI: fewer forgotten leads.

### Avoid first

- Legal document advice without a legal gate.
- Agent negotiating price or terms autonomously.
- Public listing updates without human approval.

## 3. Clinic / medical service business

### Typical process lanes

- Patient inquiry.
- Appointment booking.
- Pre-visit instructions.
- FAQ/support.
- Reminders.
- Post-visit feedback.
- Admin reporting.

### Good first pilots

**Appointment Intake Agent**

- Structures request: service, preferred time, contact, branch, urgency.
- Routes to admin.
- Does not diagnose.
- KPI: faster admin handling.

**FAQ Draft Agent**

- Answers only from approved clinic FAQ: address, preparation, schedule, price ranges if allowed.
- Escalates medical symptoms, diagnosis, and treatment decisions.
- Approval required for sensitive cases.

**Reminder Agent**

- Sends or drafts appointment/preparation reminders.
- Needs compliance/privacy check.

### Avoid first

- Medical advice.
- Diagnosis/treatment recommendation.
- Handling sensitive patient data without privacy/legal control.

## 4. Marketing agency / service agency

### Typical process lanes

- Lead intake.
- Discovery brief.
- Proposal/commercial offer.
- Project tasks.
- Content/ad production.
- Client approvals.
- Reporting.

### Good first pilots

**Brief Builder Agent**

- Takes raw client messages or call transcript.
- Produces structured brief: goals, audience, offer, assets, constraints, deadlines.
- KPI: less PM time, fewer missing inputs.

**Proposal Draft Agent**

- Uses templates + pricing rules.
- Drafts proposal sections.
- Approval by account/owner before sending.
- Risk: pricing/legal claims.

**Weekly Client Report Agent**

- Pulls metrics/screenshots/comments.
- Drafts summary: what was done, what changed, next steps, blockers.
- KPI: faster reporting, consistent client communication.

### Avoid first

- Public ad claims without review.
- Budget changes/autopublishing without approval.
- Replacing strategist judgment with generic AI outputs.

## 5. Telegram/community business

### Typical process lanes

- Content planning.
- Post drafting.
- Comments/questions.
- Paid community onboarding.
- Renewal nudges.
- Member support.
- Artifact production.

### Good first pilots

**Content Ops Agent**

- Reads raw notes, voice notes, build logs.
- Produces post drafts, digest, artifact ideas.
- Human approves before posting.
- KPI: stable publishing rhythm.

**Community Support Triage Agent**

- Classifies member questions.
- Drafts answers from rules/FAQ.
- Escalates private, payment, conflict, or sensitive cases.

**Renewal/Engagement Agent**

- Finds low-engagement moments and suggests nudges/digest.
- Must avoid spam and manipulative claims.

### Avoid first

- Fully autonomous posting in founder voice.
- Payment/refund decisions.
- Handling private psychological/finance content without boundary.

## 6. E-commerce / small retail

### Typical process lanes

- Product inquiries.
- Order status.
- Stock questions.
- Returns/exchanges.
- Reviews.
- Supplier/admin tasks.
- Sales reporting.

### Good first pilots

**Support Triage Agent**

- Classifies: status, stock, return, complaint, product question.
- Drafts answer from policy/FAQ.
- Escalates angry, high-value, or legal cases.

**Product Q&A Agent**

- Answers using product catalog + FAQ.
- Needs source of truth for price/stock.

**Daily Ops Report Agent**

- Summarizes orders, delays, complaints, low-stock items.

### Avoid first

- Refund approvals without rules/human approval.
- Price changes.
- Supplier orders without approval.

## Pattern conclusion

Across businesses the first paid pilot usually lands in one of five places:

1. Intake and qualification.
2. CRM/task hygiene.
3. Support triage and draft answers.
4. Reporting and management control.
5. Document/brief/proposal drafting.

These are sellable because they are visible, frequent, measurable, and safer than “let the agent run the business”.
